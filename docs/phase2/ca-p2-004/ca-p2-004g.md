**Status:** implemented on a draft branch for review. Synthetic-only, provisional and unreviewed. It is not a gate disposition.

**Plan:** [milestone plan](../milestone-incident/plan.md), revisions 3 and 3.1:
- §4: collection, continuity and timing;
- §5: pod identity;
- §6: RBAC and environment values;
- §7: the template catalog.

## What changed

**Sanitizer (`collector/logline.py`).** Each line is reduced in memory, on the reading thread, to its envelope (`boot`, `seq`, `synced`, `lag_ms`) plus a template from the pinned catalog `ca-log-templates/0.1-provisional` with typed fields.
- **Text values.** Every text field is an enumerated literal (`pool=checkout-db`, `key=pool.maxConnections`) or a structural identifier that is checked later: the container name against the request, and the pod UID against the watch. A free-form name field was found to let `pool=hunter2` through and was removed.
- **Lines that don't match.** An unknown template or an invalid field value keeps only the envelope. A line without a valid envelope keeps only an `invalid` code.
- **Raw text** is never stored.

**Log streams (`collector/podlogs.py`, inside `watch.run_session`).** For every container of every pod in the namespace, the collector opens:
- a history stream, which yields `seq=1`;
- an overlapping `tailLines=0` rotation stream every 5 s; an older stream is closed only once the newer one overlaps it;
- a drain stream after the final Pod probe.

After each stream closes, `GET pods/<name>` records the UID as a recheck. The collector only records; it decides nothing.

**Derivation (`app/pod_logs.py`).** This is a pure function that runs on conversion and again on replay.
- **Attribution** needs three bindings: the watch, the recheck, and the line content (a verified `STARTED` for the boot).
- **Continuity** per instance means `seq` runs from 1 to k with no conflicts, and the tail is covered: either by `STOPPED`, or by a line the handshake establishes was written after the final probe.
- **Timing uses protocol bounds only:**
  - `U` = earliest receipt;
  - `L` = the pod's creation bracket, or `O` for a `tailLines=0` stream with the handshake (line `j+1` carries `synced=j`);
  - never `O − δ`.
  
  An impossible handshake claim produces `timing_inconsistent`, which is a gap.

**Declared limits (`collector/limits.py`).** Only literal integers of declared `(container, env, catalog key)` entries are kept. They become typed `field_changes` on the rollout. Everything else is withheld and counted, and without declarations conversion is unchanged.

**Recording 0.5.** This is the 0.4 recording plus:
- a `pod_logs` manifest section (catalog version and digest, envelope, declared limits, runtime clock);
- a `pod_logs` stream with strict entry validation (a recorded line must be exactly what the sanitizer can produce).

It converts to observation kinds 0.4 and contract profile 4.

**Proven log coverage is a complete batch (M11, approved 2026-11-14).** Under contract profile 4 (batch 0.3), the log batches are `complete` (or `empty`) with a `log_sequence_continuity` basis only when two things hold:
- the Pod watch has exactly one proven segment;
- the derivation has no gap at all.

The basis states:
- the Pod watch's own anchors;
- the session and its clock;
- every instance: pod UID, container, boot, `last_seq`, and its ending (`stopped` or `drained`).

These batches hold the lines received up to the end anchor. Later lines go to a partial tail batch, as watch events after the proving probe do. Anything else keeps every log batch `partial` / `continuity_unproven`. Log continuity never makes a Pod or Deployment batch complete, and watch continuity never covers logs.

**Recording 0.6 (M12a–c, approved with safeguards on 2026-11-14).** The Pod, Deployment and list-state causes of abstention are classified narrowly, with provenance:
- status-only Deployment updates;
- generation rises that a later rollout accounts for;
- quiet pending Pods superseded by a later valid observation;
- the listed state opening the first proven batch.

Anything else stays withheld. A predefined incident window is recorded, and the verified interval is computed and reported. The plan window is not narrowed (M12d needs a contract change). [The completeness analysis](ca-p2-004g-completeness.md#implemented) has the details.

**Investigations still abstain, for reasons outside the logs.** The cause is the Pod, Deployment, ReplicaSet and Event acquisitions and the plan window. These are reproduced and analysed in [the completeness analysis](ca-p2-004g-completeness.md), with a proposal (M12a–d) that is not implemented.

**Kind.**
- **Incident namespace.** `ca-incident` runs a standard-library workload with the envelope and the write-after-append handshake. The workload reads its own pod-log directory through a read-only `hostPath`.
- **Grant.** The collector gets `get pods/log` in `ca-incident` only.
- **New CI job `collector-logs-kind`.** It checks RBAC and runs a real `DB_POOL_MAX` 16 → 4 rollout while logs are collected. It then checks coverage, the typed change, the symptom brackets and leaks, and replays offline and in PostgreSQL.

**Workload image.** `python:3.13.9-slim-trixie`, pinned by digest, `ci-only`, inventoried, SBOM regenerated. CI checks that every incident pod ran exactly that digest.
- **Approval.** The product owner approved it on 2026-11-14 for the disposable kind environment only. That approval is not a specialist legal review.
- **Licensing.** PSF-2.0 is CPython's license. The full image also contains Debian packages and other layers under their own licenses, some of them copyleft, and full-image license clearance stays pending.
- **Why not BusyBox.** BusyBox is GPL-2.0, and the license policy refuses it without an approved review ([plan, M8](../milestone-incident/plan.md#decisions)).

## Tests

**Sanitizer** (`tests/collector/test_logline.py`, 8 tests):
- typed fields;
- the envelope-only fallback;
- invalid shapes;
- planted needles in every position;
- enumerated text fields.

**Derivation** (`tests/collector/test_pod_logs_derive.py`, 23 tests):
- **Timing sensitivity.** Write-to-append delays of 0, 0.5, 1.9, 2.1, 5 and 30 s, with and without the handshake. Every computed lower bound is at or before the true write time.
- **The rejected δ rule.** With a delay above 2 s, it would place the bound too late. The product never computes it.
- **Handshake faults:** a missing claim, a skipped or wrong claim, a line received before the line it claims to follow, and out-of-order delivery.
- **Continuity faults:** a disconnect without overlap, late attachment, a dropped line, a conflicting duplicate, an unparseable line, a partial line, a restart without `STOPPED`, a missing instance, lines after `STOPPED`, a missing drain, a pod never collected, and a failed open.
- **Identity faults:** name reuse, a stream opened while the name meant another pod, `STARTED` naming another pod or container, and recheck mismatch, 404 or missing.

**Declared limits** (`tests/collector/test_limits.py`, 8 tests):
- only literal integers;
- `secretKeyRef`, `configMapKeyRef`, `fieldRef` and `resourceFieldRef` are withheld;
- malformed integers (`" 4"`, `"04"`, `"1e3"`, `"-4"`, fullwidth `"４"`, `"4\n"`, `""`, 7 digits, `"4.0"`, `"0x4"`) are withheld;
- declarations in another container are ignored;
- credential-like declarations are refused;
- a tampered projected value is refused.

**Completeness characterization** (`tests/collector/test_rollout_completeness.py`, 3 tests): pins the four non-log causes of abstention.

**End to end** (`tests/collector/test_pod_log_session.py`, 8 tests). Pod logs are collected inside a watch session against a fake `pods/log` server:
- a clean run proves coverage as a complete batch whose basis binds the session, its clock and every instance; Pod batches keep `watch_continuity`;
- a forged basis (`last_seq` changed) is refused on replay;
- a dropped line keeps every log batch partial with no basis;
- planted log and env values are never written;
- denied, dropped-line, garbage-line, name-reuse and partial-line runs all abstain with the expected gaps.

**Existing suites.** Unchanged and passing: contracts, engine (the six pinned digests), app, API, collector (121 tests) and the three truth pins.
