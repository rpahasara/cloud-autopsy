# CA-P2-002e: observation kinds 0.4 and contract profile 4 (record)

**Status:** implemented on a draft branch for review. It is synthetic-only, provisional and unreviewed, and is not a gate disposition.

**Plan:** [milestone plan, revision 2](../milestone-incident/plan.md), decisions M5 and M6, and [§13](../milestone-incident/plan.md#scope).

## What changed

**Observation kinds `ca-observation-kinds/0.4-provisional`.** These are every 0.3 kind, unchanged, with `log_line` widened:
- **Source kind.** It may come from a `loki` source (as in 0.1) or from a `kubernetes_api` source, because `pods/log` is a Kubernetes API subresource. The source registry (`ca-source-registry/0.1-provisional`) is unchanged.
- **Time.** It may carry an `observed` time, with the same collector-clock bracket meaning as 0.3.
- **Attributes.** It may omit `message`, and may carry the sequence envelope `container`, `boot` (8 lowercase hex) and `seq` (1 or more).
- **Rules:**
  - a `kubernetes_api` log line must carry the whole envelope and must **never** carry `message`, so free text read from a container cannot be stored;
  - a `loki` line still needs `message`;
  - a partial envelope is refused from either source.
- **Labels.** A record carries the earliest version under which it is valid. A 0.1-shaped `loki` line keeps its 0.1 label and its bytes. Every 0.4-only shape is labelled 0.4, and is refused under 0.1–0.3, including when relabelled.

**Contract profile 4.** Observation kinds 0.4 with acquisition batch **0.3** (M11). Profiles 1–3 are unchanged.

**Acquisition batch `ca-acquisition-batch/0.3-provisional` (M11, approved 2026-11-14).** Batch 0.3 keeps every 0.2 field and rule, and adds a second basis kind for complete and empty batches of container logs, `log_sequence_continuity`.

- **What it claims.** Every container instance that the proven Pod watch segment reported is continuous over that segment's verified interval.
- **What it contains:**
  - the Pod watch anchors;
  - the collection session and its own clock;
  - each instance's pod UID, container, boot and `last_seq`;
  - its verified ending: `stopped`, or `drained` (a line established as written after the final probe).
- **What a batch with this basis may hold.** Only log lines of listed instances, within their proven sequence.
- **No crossover between the two basis kinds.** Under 0.3 a `watch_continuity` batch may hold no log line. So log continuity never stands in for Pod or Deployment coverage, and the reverse never happens either.
- **How claims are checked.** Coherence is checked by the contract. Truth is checked by re-deriving the basis from the recording on replay.
- **Earlier versions.** Batches 0.1 and 0.2 are unchanged, and 0.2 still refuses the new basis kind.

**Migration `0009_pod_log_kinds.sql`.** It inserts two supported-version rows, acquisition batch 0.3 and observation kinds 0.4, and widens the batch version check from 0.1–0.2 to 0.1–0.3. Every stored row satisfies both the old and the new check. Migrations `0001`–`0008` are unchanged.

**Converter guard.** `k8s_recording.convert` refuses kinds 0.4 until the collector slice (CA-P2-004g) adds a recording that produces pod logs.

  - other kinds unchanged;
  - profile 4.
