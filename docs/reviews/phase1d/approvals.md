| Reviewer role | Required review | Independence and approval record |
|---|---|---|
| Product / requirements authority | Scope, differentiation hypothesis, lifecycle periods, benchmark purpose, funding/Phase 2 scope and all eight product dispositions | Named owner with dated document/commit and exact decision scope; repository access alone grants no authority |
| Architecture / distributed-systems reviewer | Component contracts, timestamp uncertainty, deterministic hashing, freeze/replay states and integration boundaries | A qualified person other than the sole design author; may combine DB specialization if competent, with conflicts recorded |

| Database / privacy-lifecycle specialist | Same-run integrity, least privilege, transaction races, expiry/deletion, safe tombstones and export inventory | Can be the architecture or security reviewer if qualified, but cannot replace required independent second-person challenge |
| Evaluation / benchmarking reviewer | Ground truth, scorer/rubric, held-out secrecy, comparison fairness, denominators and uncertainty | Cannot solely approve ground truth or scorer they authored; evaluate blinded outputs before unblinding tool identity |
| Open-source licensing/commercial reviewer | Exact shipped artifacts, dependency licenses/NOTICE, AGPL/MPL obligations, model weights/terms and trademarks | Qualified licensing specialist/legal counsel for intended distribution/service model; engineering research is not legal clearance |
| Risk Acceptance Authority | Any proposed residual-risk disposition and changes to a gate | Named authorized person, separate recorded decision; acceptance does not automatically waive a gate or authorize external egress |

No reviewers are nominated, contacted or recorded as approving by this package. Several specialties can be combined in a small team, but independence cannot be collapsed into the single author/maintainer. Product approval alone cannot supply missing security, licensing or evaluation evidence.
